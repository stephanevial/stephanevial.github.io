**Stéphane Vial**, Ph.D., is a Full Professor at the School of Design of the Université du Québec à Montréal, a regular researcher at the IUSMM Research Centre and the founder of Mentallys Inc.

He holds a PhD in philosophy, a degree in clinical psychology and a habilitation to supervise research in design, and teaches, among other things, the use of artificial intelligence in research, writing and creative work.

The author of several books, including *Being and the Screen: How the Digital Changes Perception* (MIT Press), he explores how technologies transform our ways of perceiving, thinking and acting.
