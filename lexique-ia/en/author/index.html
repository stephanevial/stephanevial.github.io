The author

Stéphane Vial

Stéphane Vial is a Full Professor at the School of Design of the Université du Québec à Montréal, with a PhD in philosophy and a degree in clinical psychology.

A lover of words, he is the author of several reference works on design and digital culture, including Being and the Screen: How the Digital Changes Perception (MIT Press) and the Vocabulaire du design (PUF, in French).
